WHO WE ARE

Supporting Staff Through Technology

The National Drought Management Authority (NDMA) works to coordinate drought management and strengthen drought resilience in Kenya.

The NDMA ICT Support Portal is designed to provide staff with a central place to report technical issues and request help with computers, internet connectivity, email, printers and other ICT services.

By organising support requests in one place, the portal aims to make communication between staff and the ICT support team clearer and more efficient.
