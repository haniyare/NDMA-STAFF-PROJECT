About Our Helpdesk

Empowering NDMA Staff with Self-Help IT Solutions

🎯 Our Mission

At National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), every minute counts. When disaster strikes, ICT systems must work. This Helpdesk was created to help NDMA staff solve common IT problems in under 2 minutes without waiting for ICT support.

No more waiting for printer fix, WiFi issues, or Outlook errors — just open the helpdesk and solve it yourself!

💡 Why We Built This?

✔️ 250+ Staff face small IT issues daily
✔️ ICT Team is busy with critical disaster systems
✔️ 70% issues are simple and repeatable (printer, WiFi, PC slow)
✔️ Solution: A beautiful self-help portal — built for NDMA by NDMA
