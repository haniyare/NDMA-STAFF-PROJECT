Check the printer's power, paper, ink or toner, and connection. Confirm that the correct printer is selected. If the issue remains, contact ICT support with the error message or printer details.

Be careful with unexpected attachments, urgent payment requests, unfamiliar links, and messages asking for passwords. Do not click suspicious links or reply with confidential information. Report suspicious messages through your organisation's approved security process.
Check your internet connection and sign-in details. If access still fails, contact authorised ICT support. Never send your password by email or message.
An ICT support ticket is a record of a technical problem or service request. It helps the support team document the issue, track progress, and record the resolution.
Use your organisation's approved helpdesk process or contact the authorised ICT support team. Include a clear description, the affected device or service, and any error message. Do not include passwords.
