How do I reset my NDMA email password? ▼
Contact ICT via ticket or visit Room 203. Password reset within 30 minutes. You need your Staff ID.
Why am I not receiving external emails? ▼
Check Spam/Junk folder. If not there, your mailbox may be full. Clear old emails or contact ICT to increase quota.
How to setup NDMA email on my phone? ▼
Use Outlook App > Add Account > Exchange > email: yourname@ndma.go.ke > Server: outlook.office365.com
Wi-Fi connected but no internet? ▼
1. Forget network and reconnect 2. Restart laptop 3. Check if other staff have same issue. If yes, it's a central outage - we are fixing.
What is the Wi-Fi password for guests? ▼
Guest Wi-Fi: NDMA-GUEST. Password changes weekly. Request from ICT Helpdesk with visitor name and ID.
How to get VPN for working from home? ▼
Email ict@ndma.go.ke with Staff ID, department, and HOD approval. VPN credentials will be sent securely.
Internet is very slow in my office block? ▼
Peak hours 10am-12pm may be slow. If persistent, raise a ticket with your floor & room number. We will check the access point.
My computer is not powering on? ▼
Check power cable and extension. Try different socket. If beeping sound, note the beep pattern and call Ext 231.
How to request a new laptop / printer? ▼
Download Equipment Request Form from intranet, get HOD signature, submit to ICT. Processing 2-3 working days.
Printer shows offline error? ▼
1. Check USB/Network cable 2. Restart printer 3. On your PC, go to Control Panel > Devices and Printers > Right-click > Use Printer Online.
My projector is not displaying? ▼
Press Windows + P and select Duplicate. Check HDMI/VGA cable. For boardroom projectors, use remote Input > HDMI 1.
I clicked a suspicious email link, what to do? ▼
IMMEDIATELY disconnect from internet, DO NOT enter password, and call ICT Security Team Ext 231. Change your password after.
How to protect my files from ransomware? ▼
Always backup to OneDrive, don't open .exe from email, keep antivirus updated. ICT runs daily backup at 6pm.
Is it safe to use personal flash disks? ▼
Scan with antivirus first. Personal USBs are blocked on some systems. Use NDMA OneDrive for file transfer instead.
Who do I contact for urgent help? ▼
For critical outage/security: Call Ext 231 or visit 2nd Floor ICT Office. WhatsApp Helpdesk: 0712 345 678. 8am-5pm Mon-Fri.
